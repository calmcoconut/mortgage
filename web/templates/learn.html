Mortgage Compass Methodology

Understand the math behind financing cost calculations, points break-even, and screening rules.

1. Fixed-Rate Amortization Formula

Monthly principal and interest (P&I) payment is calculated using exact Decimal precision:

r = Annual Rate / 12
P = LoanAmount × [ r(1 + r)ⁿ ] / [ (1 + r)ⁿ - 1 ]

In the final month, the principal payment is explicitly reconciled against the remaining balance so that the final loan balance reaches exactly $0.00.

2. Financing Cost vs Cash-Flow Burden

Mortgage Compass isolates the true economic cost of financing from equity accumulation and property-level taxes/insurance:

Net Upfront = (Points % × LoanAmount) - LenderCredits + LenderFees + UpfrontMI
FinancingCost(h) = NetUpfront + Σ(Interest_i) + Σ(MonthlyMI_i) [for i = 1 to h]
  • Principal repayment is excluded because every dollar paid toward principal builds home equity.
  • Property taxes, homeowners insurance, and HOA dues are excluded from financing cost because they are property-specific rather than lender loan terms.

3. Points Break-Even Calculation

The break-even month represents the exact month $h$ when the cumulative interest saved from a lower rate surpasses the upfront cost of discount points:

BreakEvenMonth = min { h | FinancingCost_candidate(h) < FinancingCost_baseline(h) }

4. Screening Sanity Check

The screening tool derives Debt-to-Income (DTI) from your proposed housing payment and recurring debts against your gross income. It evaluates standard Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac (Conventional), FHA, VA, and USDA parameters for planning sanity checks. It is explicitly not an automated underwriting system (AUS) decision or mortgage approval.
